1. About TowingIreland.ie

TowingIreland.ie ("the Platform", "we", "us", "our") is an online heavy vehicle recovery request service operated in Ireland. The Platform allows users ("you", "the Customer") to submit recovery requests for heavy vehicles, which we review and arrange with independent recovery operators ("Recovery Operators").

TowingIreland.ie acts solely as an intermediary platform. We do not provide towing or vehicle recovery services ourselves, and we are not party to any contract formed between a Customer and a Recovery Operator.

Vehicles we cover

The Platform is for heavy vehicles only: vehicles with a gross vehicle weight (GVW) of at least 3,500kg and no more than 44,000kg, and at least 18 feet (approx. 5.5m) in length. This includes rigid trucks, HGVs, articulated lorries and tractor units, buses, coaches, tippers, specialist vehicles, plant and agricultural machinery, and motorhomes, horseboxes and trailers over 3,500kg. Cars, small vans, motorcycles and other vehicles outside this range are not covered, and requests for them will be declined.
